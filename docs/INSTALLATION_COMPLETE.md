## 🔧 How to Use

### Viewing Locations
1. Click "Manage Hours" tab
2. See grid of locations
3. Click "View & Manage" on any location

### Editing Hours
1. Go to "Regular Hours" tab
2. Click "Edit Hours"
3. Modify times for each day
4. Click "Save Changes"

### Adding Time Slots
1. Go to "Time Slots" tab
2. Click "+ Add Time Slot"
3. Fill in shift details
4. Click "Add Slot"

### Adding Holidays
1. Go to "Holiday Exceptions" tab
2. Click "+ Add Holiday"
3. Pick date and name
4. Click "Add Holiday"
